## Core Theoretical & Computational Models

1. **Dynamic Optimization & Growth Theory:**
   - Calculus of variations, Pontryagin maximum principle, Hamiltonians, and Bellman equations.
   - Neoclassical Growth Model (Ramsey-Cass-Koopmans) in discrete and continuous time.
   - Diamond Overlapping Generations (OLG) model and social security dynamics.
2. **Real Business Cycle (RBC) Theory:**
   - Kydland-Prescott benchmark stochastic model with technology shocks.
   - Log-linearization around the steady state.
   - Numerical simulation, calibration to stylized facts, and Impulse Response Functions (IRFs).
3. **Labor Market Search & Matching:**
   - McCall search model with reservation wages.
   - Mortensen-Pissarides matching function, Beveridge curve, and equilibrium unemployment dynamics.
4. **Firm Heterogeneity & Resource Misallocation:**
   - Hsieh & Klenow (2009) framework: idiosyncratic capital and output distortions, monopolistic competition, and aggregate TFP loss.
5. **Consumption, Savings & Precautionary Motives:**
   - Hall random walk hypothesis, buffer stock savings, and permanent income under liquidity constraints.
